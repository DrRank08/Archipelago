## How This Randomizer Works ##

This is a Manual AP, meaning there is no modification to the game itself.
Instead, you use a Manual Client that is connected to the server but not the game, and manually check off each location as you go.
Basically, it's the honour system.

## Setup ##

You can play this randomizer with any version of Finding Nemo for PS2, Xbox or GameCube. This includes using the original disc on console, or emulation.
The only additional piece of software you need is the apworld.

Before you start, you'll need to input the Open Levels cheat code, which unlocks every level in the game including Bonus Levels.
To use the Open Levels code, go to the title screen and input the following buttons:
- PS2: Triangle Triangle Triangle Square Square Circle Square Triangle Circle Square Triangle Square Triangle Square Triangle Circle Triangle Triangle
- Xbox: Y Y Y X X B X Y B X Y X Y X Y B Y Y
- GCN: Y Y Y B B X B Y X B Y B Y B Y X Y Y
If you did it right, you'll see the word "Cheat!" appear.
After that, boot up a New Game, pause, and enable the "Open Levels" cheat.
You can also load a save file with every level beaten and 0/60 Starfish, to enable skippable cutscenes. However, you will still need the Open Levels cheat to open the Bonus Levels.

After generating your seed as normal, open the Manual Client from the Archipelago Launcher.
Ensure that the game name is set to "Manual_Finding Nemo_DrRank", then connect to the server.
It is also recommended that you install the Universal Tracker (downloaded separately), to more easily identify available locations.
## Goal ##

You have three options for your objective:
- Fishing Net Rescue: Complete the level Fishing Net Rescue. This requires only its Level Key and Nemo.
- All Levels: Complete all 18 levels and unlock the credits. This requires all 18 Level Keys, all 3 Characters, the Luxo Ball, and the Red Pebble.
- 100%: Same as All Levels, but you also need to collect a certain number of Starfish. You can adjust how many Starfish you need in the options.

## Items & Rules ##

Client Management:
In the Client, you will find the "Manual" tab, where you can view all your current items and available location checks.
If you're using the Universal Tracker, any checks that are in logic (that is, currently reachable) will be highlighted in green.
Once you complete a location check, click on its button in the client to award its item.
Checks exist for the following scenarios:
- Clearing Levels (that is, reaching the end)
- Completing Starfish objectives:
	- Bubble Ring Bonus (swim through all Bubble Rings)
	- Hero Bonus (defeat all killable enemies using Bubbles)
	- Pebble Bonus (carry the Red, Green and Blue Pebbles to their matching platforms; requires the Pebble items)
	- Bounce Bonus (bounce on every tile during a bouncing section)
	- Race Bonus (win the race)
	- Luxo Ball Bonus (send the Luxo Ball through all Hoops; requires the Luxo Ball item)
	- Slide Puzzle (unscramble the image)
	- Pairs (match all the card pairs)
	- Simon Says (copy the sequence of shapes that the Moonfish form)

Characters:
- Nemo
- Marlin
- Dory
You begin the run with one of these unlocked at random.
When a character is locked, you may not provide any controller inputs as them other than Start.
In levels where you can manually change characters by tagging them, you may not tag a locked character.

Levels:
There is one Level item for each of the 18 levels in the game.
You begin the run with one level unlocked at random, being one that your starting character can complete.
You can only enter a level that you have unlocked.
Completing a level counts as a check, as does completing a Starfish objective within that level.

Starfish:
There are 60 Starfish in the game, all of them level-dependant.
Some levels have more Starfish than others; this is part of the game itself.
Collecting at least one Starfish for any given level allows you to access its Bonus Level.
To play a Bonus Level, highlight its base level, press Up, and then press A (X on PS2).
Bonus Levels award two checks: one for the Starfish, and one for the Bonus itself.
There are also checks for every 10th Starfish you collect in total.

Misc:
- Red Pebble
- Green Pebble
- Blue Pebble
- Luxo Ball
These items represent interactible objects you can find in the game.
You may not interact with any of these objects in any level until you unlock them.
The five pebbles in The Plan count as Red Pebbles.

Filler:
All remaining items are filler and have no functional purpose.
The Shells item would add 10 Shells to your score, but this cannot be implemented until a modded version is created.
The other filler items exist purely for flavour.
